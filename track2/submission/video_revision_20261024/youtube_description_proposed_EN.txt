We connect public-data reanalyses and evidence audits to drug-repurposing priorities for BUB1B-associated mosaic variegated aneuploidy. The proposal distinguishes RNA abundance, protein abundance and functional rescue, and outlines an exact-variant experiment with prespecified controls and decision criteria.

In the October 22 reproduction audit, four checked computational reanalysis outputs matched archived checksums in a clean environment using public processed inputs. This is computational reproduction, not biological or clinical validation. No exact-genotype pharmacological rescue is established. Wet-lab validation remains future work.
