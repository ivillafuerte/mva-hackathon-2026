Which drug hypotheses deserve the next experiment? BUB1B encodes BUBR1, a protein involved in chromosome segregation. Our virtual lab connects public evidence to candidate decisions, making the supporting findings, contradictions and remaining uncertainties visible.

The two variants raise different questions. L737 stop introduces premature termination; nonsense-mediated decay is predicted, not measured for this exact variant. For N1002K, stability and residual function remain unresolved. Preserving RNA or increasing protein abundance therefore cannot, by itself, establish functional rescue.

One reanalysis challenged an RNA-increase rationale for sirolimus. In normal human fibroblasts, a BUB1B-spanning RNA feature was forty-one percent lower after five days of rapamycin than in proliferative controls. This is a limited RNA observation, not a protein or rescue measurement. It keeps the hypothesis conditional.

Readthrough evidence also has boundaries. Twelve nearby sequence contexts did not provide a measurement for the exact L737 stop variant. Separately, published BUBR1 studies show why abundance and checkpoint measurements cannot replace chromosome-segregation tests. Our decision rule requires evidence at each step, with the model and its limitations attached.

Amlexanox remains a conditional research candidate: functional evidence in another gene is counterbalanced by negative neuronal findings and toxicity in a different model. Montelukast remains an NMD-modulation hypothesis, dependent on whether the exact truncated protein retains useful function. Gentamicin plus mefloquine remains experimental. Immunotherapy is deprioritized for disease rescue: tumor control does not establish correction of BUB1B dysfunction. None is a treatment recommendation.

The working website contains eleven records: four reanalyses, six evidence audits and one software test, alongside four quantitative charts. Each record separates findings, sources, limitations and the next question. Interactive illustrations explain mechanisms; they do not run experiments or calculate drug efficacy.

The decisive next step is exact-variant validation against matched controls: measure allele-specific RNA and protein, checkpoint response, and segregation errors per completed division, while tracking viability and proliferation. A candidate advances only with functional improvement beyond cytostasis. This evidence-to-decision framework is designed to support future evaluation across other variant mechanisms.

Our contribution is a testable research path. No exact-genotype pharmacological rescue is established. Wet-lab validation remains future work.
