### Approach from variant to medication — row 8

The rapamycin RNA-feature observation constrains a sirolimus RNA-increase rationale. Amlexanox and montelukast remain conditional hypotheses; gentamicin + mefloquine remains experimental. The proposed next step is exact-variant validation with WT/matched controls, viability and proliferation tracking. The October 24 sensitivity audit re-reads verified public processed inputs, reports finite-set influence diagnostics and preserves unresolved proliferation confounding. ### Method abstract — row 17, fewer than 500 words

We developed an allele-aware evidence-to-decision workflow for BUB1B-associated mosaic variegated aneuploidy using the challenge-nominated L737\*/N1002K pair. Earlier public sequence and structural computations established transcript context and model geometry without proving variant function. The October evidence workspace contains four reanalyses, six qualitative evidence audits and one synthetic software test, with four charts. It does not run biological experiments or calculate drug efficacy.

Two observations constrain prioritization. A BUB1B-spanning RNA feature was 41.43% lower in a limited normal-fibroblast rapamycin reanalysis, not a protein or rescue endpoint. Nearby readthrough contexts did not include an exact L737\* measurement. Amlexanox is a conditional other-gene-supported hypothesis with contradictory neuronal evidence and unresolved current product eligibility. Montelukast is an NMD-modulation hypothesis contingent on useful exact-truncation function. Gentamicin plus mefloquine remains experimental; immunotherapy tumor outcomes are not disease rescue.

The proposed decisive experiment measures allele-specific RNA and protein, checkpoint response and segregation errors per completed division against matched controls, while tracking viability and proliferation. No exact-genotype pharmacological rescue is established. The public snapshot and final video brief were cross-checked. Recovered October scripts reproduced four deterministic output artifacts from public processed inputs, without using the snapshot as input. Upstream limits include reused published permutation results, archived annotation and a small prediction extract. September allele computations remain separately documented. The October 24 post-hoc sensitivity audit reports 12 nearest PTC rows across 8 unique contexts, finite leave-out ranges, feature-selection checks and unadjusted proliferation-state comparisons; these do not add biological replication or establish causal specificity. Systemic exposure, product eligibility and the organizer-prescribed dataset citation remain unresolved. No new wet-laboratory efficacy evidence or patient treatment recommendation is presented.
