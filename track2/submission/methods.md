## Method abstract — row 17, fewer than 500 words

We developed an allele-aware research workflow for BUB1B-associated mosaic variegated aneuploidy using the challenge-nominated L737*/N1002K pair. Public reference sequences established variant identity and premature-stop context. Human AlphaFold coordinates were analyzed for confidence, solvent accessibility, local contacts and exploratory lysine geometry; these computations do not estimate folding energy or prove pathogenicity. Primary literature was curated to distinguish exact-variant evidence, other BUBR1 variants, pathway evidence and inference. Regulatory labels anchored a shortlist of market-approved medications.

The proposed experimental reference is restoration of WT BUBR1, with amount, cell coverage and timing measured separately. Sirolimus and anakinra address conditional downstream tissue mechanisms; everolimus is a comparator; nicotinic acid tests a lower-confidence NAD–SIRT2 rescue hypothesis; gentamicin serves as a readthrough benchmark. Candidates require functional validation and pharmacologically plausible exposure. New segregation errors per completed division are distinguished from cell death, cytostasis and shifts in clone abundance. Prenatal mRNA replacement is retained as a future extension rather than the nominated repurposed product.

Strengths are explicit falsification criteria, computational reproducibility and separation of causal rescue from tissue protection. Limitations include absent exact-genotype functional data, unresolved phase, model-dependent structural inference, incomplete access to some publications/datasets, and no new wet-laboratory efficacy evidence. The work generates testable hypotheses rather than treatment recommendations.
