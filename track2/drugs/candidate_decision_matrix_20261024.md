# BUB1B candidate decision matrix

**Prepared October 24, 2026 | Stackfire Networks | ivillafuerte.** No exact-genotype pharmacological rescue is established. Priorities are experimental branches, not an efficacy ranking or treatment plan. Product findings below were already public in main commit `0cb71c4`; they are not new achievements of this update. Competition eligibility remains a separate determination.

| **Sirolimus**. Mechanistic comparator, not current rescue lead. Product-level re-nomination and eligibility not established in this matrix. | No exact-genotype rescue. **Shared GO rule:** predefine independent biological units, primary functional endpoint, meaningful effect, precision and toxicity/exposure bounds. Inconclusive is not GO. Checkpoint-only benefit is not segregation rescue. No candidate passes these gates yet.
