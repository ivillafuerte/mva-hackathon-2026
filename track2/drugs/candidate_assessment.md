## Ranking method

Apply an eligibility gate before scientific ranking: identify a specific currently listed market-approved product, distinguish its approved indications from MVA, and preserve unresolved regulatory questions. US prescription labels are used as auditable approval precedents; worldwide availability and eligibility in every jurisdiction are not inferred. A DailyMed entry alone is not sufficient if it belongs to an unapproved product; inspect its marketing category and indication. Recheck status immediately before submission.

Then assess the following 13 dimensions without inventing calibrated numerical scores. Unknown evidence remains unknown and is not scored as a neutral safety result.

## Product-level regulatory anchors

- **Sirolimus:** [US authorized-generic label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=5908cd1a-fc5a-462f-99ed-1d8983e253c9), label page updated March 26, 2026. Approved contexts include renal-transplant rejection prophylaxis and lymphangioleiomyomatosis. The transplant indication specifies age 13 years and older. Pediatric vascular-anomaly research is separate: PMID 26783326, DOI [10.1542/peds.2015-3257](https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2015-3257). It provides experience, not a safety guarantee for this child. Review CYP3A/P-gp interactions against an actual medication list before clinical translation; none is available here.
- **Anakinra:** [Kineret label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=d9d74915-6606-4570-9c52-c4001d3177de), revision October 2025, BLA 103950. Pediatric NOMID and DIRA precedents do not establish an MVA indication. Renal elimination matters if renal impairment is documented; nephrocalcinosis alone does not establish impairment.
- **Everolimus:** [US oral-suspension label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=5b809dfe-c076-07c5-207e-178c3f0d32d3), ANDA-listed product. Pediatric TSC-associated SEGA from age one and partial-onset seizures from age two are distinct approved settings. Mean half-life is approximately 30 hours, but tissue recovery cannot be calculated from plasma half-life alone. Formulations are not casually interchangeable.
- **Nicotinic acid:** [Quagen extended-release niacin label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=249e27ac-e1e7-42dc-84f6-cd32fd1d9dee), ANDA-listed product; active ingredient explicitly nicotinic acid. Approved lipid indications establish repurposing precedent, not safety in MVA. The page header, SPL publication date and internal revision date differ; use the record identity and retrieval date rather than claiming one unambiguous update date. Do not substitute immediate-, sustained- and extended-release formulations or supplements. Pediatric effectiveness/safety is unestablished. Its nutrient identity does not remove pharmacological toxicity.
- **Gentamicin:** [US injection label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=60ca1487-392d-4721-aa45-3de38722896c). Antibacterial approval does not support prolonged treatment for readthrough. Its major renal and auditory risks are central to the decision to retain it as a laboratory benchmark only.

## Advancement gates

**Sirolimus:** establish a relevant mTOR/secretory phenotype. Compare transient and sustained exposure using pS6 and other appropriate target-engagement readouts, actual recovery of protein synthesis and tissue function, and errors per division. Static LC3 abundance is insufficient to establish autophagic flux. The published brief-exposure fibroblast experiment retained partial pathway suppression after withdrawal; do not advertise a proven toxicity-free recovery window. Intermittent mouse studies provide a rationale for comparison, not a pediatric schedule (PMID 26463117; DOI [10.1111/acel.12405](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12405)).

**Anakinra:** establish IL-1R dependence with an orthogonal perturbation. Measure cell-surface IL1A and downstream signaling, not only soluble IL-1. Control drug carryover when transferring conditioned medium to muscle cells. Require tissue benefit and preserved arrest of damaged cells. A direct comparison can favor either drug; specificity alone does not prove superior safety.

**Nicotinic acid:** require intracellular NAD response and evidence that any BUBR1 improvement depends on the proposed SIRT2 pathway. Compare abundance-matched protein function. Determine whether the effect involves N1002K, a readthrough product or another route. Do not assume that raising NAD always activates a specific sirtuin in the relevant compartment. Stop if only metabolism/viability improves while segregation remains defective. See main report R8–R9; the latter is adult evidence and has a linked erratum, not genotype-specific evidence.

**Readthrough:** use intact transcript processing and endogenous NMD, exact stop context, orthogonal protein detection, and functional tests of candidate inserted residues. No new measurement from the large readthrough library has been recovered for this exact stop. The library study is PMID 39174735, DOI [10.1038/s41588-024-01878-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-024-01878-5); do not substitute a library-wide average for L737* efficacy.

## Exposure translation

This dossier does not assign patient doses or claim an established pharmacological window. Before advancing a hit, compare measured free extracellular and intracellular exposure with clinically documented exposure for the exact product. Account for protein binding, metabolites, time above an effective level, formulation, tissue penetration and drug interactions. An active concentration that is unattainable without unacceptable toxicity is a negative translational result, even if its mechanism is attractive.

Amlexanox and ataluren are not nominated without current product/jurisdiction eligibility verification. NMN/NR and experimental peptides do not inherit eligibility from niacin or another marketed platform. Proteasome and HSP90 perturbations are mechanistic controls, not automatically therapeutic candidates. A senolytic combination is not selected merely because genetic removal of senescent cells helped a BubR1 mouse model.

## Historical decision — September 10 (superseded)

Develop sirolimus and anakinra as separate conditional tissue-protection hypotheses, with everolimus as a pharmacological comparator. Keep nicotinic acid in a lower-confidence molecular-rescue arm and gentamicin as a benchmark. The intervention must earn progression through the functional gates. No compound has passed them in the present project.
