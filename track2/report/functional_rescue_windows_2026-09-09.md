# Functional rescue windows: concept record

Original analysis September 9, 2026; English edition September 10, 2026. See the main report and development plan for the current proposal.

## Scientific objective

Determine how much functional BUBR1, in what fraction of cells and for how long, reduces new chromosome-segregation errors. WT BUB1B supplementation provides a proposed experimental reference. It does not repair the underlying alleles or necessarily normalize existing karyotypes.

Patient-cell checkpoint rescue supports this target (PMID 20516114; DOI [10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-09-4319](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-09-4319), Fig. 1D). Sustained transgenic expression in mice provides another precedent (PMID 23242215; DOI [10.1038/ncb2643](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncb2643)). Neither proves prenatal replacement efficacy in L737*/N1002K.

## Temporal design — OUR INFERENCE

Compare early/sustained, delayed/sustained, early/transient and no-rescue conditions. Measure baseline state, new errors per division, cell survival, coverage and tissue function. Follow withdrawal to distinguish transient protein action from persistent benefit. An established aneuploid lineage may remain aneuploid despite improved future segregation. A lower abnormal-cell fraction can reflect selection rather than repair.

A constructed trans model is an assumption until the patient's phase is directly established. N1002K abundance/function and L737* RNA/product identity remain missing biological data. No full patient genome is required for public-reference analysis, and none was newly ingested.

## Prenatal extension — SPECULATIVE FUTURE DIRECTION

Fetal delivery studies in mice include hepatic production of secreted proteins (PMID 33523869; DOI 10.1126/sciadv.aba1028), heart/diaphragm/muscle delivery (PMID 36844366; DOI 10.1016/j.bioactmat.2023.02.011) and brain editing (PMID 39445691; DOI 10.1021/acsnano.4c05169). A prenatal Pompe enzyme-replacement case was followed by postnatal treatment (PMID 36351280; DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2200587). These are platform/timing precedents, not BUBR1 rescue.

Persistent Cre recombination is not persistent therapeutic mRNA. BUBR1 needs intracellular action; secretion by the liver is not a general solution. Delivery coverage, duration, maternal/fetal effects, repeat administration and oncology need separate study. Prenatal diagnosis may follow earlier embryonic injury; no optimal gestational window is established.

An experimental progerin-targeting peptide affected BUBR1 in HGPS (PMID 37118121; DOI 10.1038/s43587-023-00361-w; abstract reviewed). It neither establishes that mechanism in MVA nor provides an approved repurposing candidate.

## Track 2 bridge

Use WT rescue to calibrate approved-drug experiments. Readthrough and stabilization require direct functional tests; sirolimus/anakinra address conditional tissue mechanisms. A new BUB1B mRNA product is outside ordinary Track 2 eligibility, even if another mRNA platform is approved. No clinical or fetal treatment is proposed.

Bibliographic metadata for seven primary articles can be refreshed with `python track2/evidence/fetch_rescue_sources.py`. Matching identifiers does not validate efficacy. The project has not performed the proposed cell or animal experiments. Distribute this record with the main report and its required acknowledgment.
