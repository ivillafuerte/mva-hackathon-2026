# Anakinra: historical alternative hypothesis

Original hypothesis date: September 8, 2026. English edition: September 10, 2026. Current ranking is in the main Track 2 report; this note does not preserve an earlier preference for anakinra over sirolimus.

## Hypothesis

Test whether IL-1 receptor blockade reduces a harmful secretory state and preserves muscle function in an appropriate BUB1B model. Atrophy and poor growth do not by themselves establish IL-1 activation or senescence. No anakinra efficacy in the exact genotype has been demonstrated here, and no increase in BUBR1 or correction of aneuploidy is claimed.

## Evidence chain

- DIRECT-BUBR1, another model: genetic elimination of p16-positive cells improved selected phenotypes in BubR1 hypomorphic mice. This was an INK-ATTAC experiment, not anakinra treatment. PMID 22048312; DOI [10.1038/nature10600](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature10600), Fig. 2, 4.
- RELATED-MVA-VARIANT: tissue and secretory phenotypes differ between alleles. PMID 31738183; DOI [10.1172/JCI126863](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/126863), Fig. 8–9.
- PATHWAY EVIDENCE: recombinant IL-1 receptor antagonist and orthogonal perturbations reduced IL6/IL8 in senescent human fibroblasts. The reagent is not claimed to be the marketed Kineret formulation. PMID 19805069; DOI [10.1073/pnas.0905299106](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0905299106), Fig. 2–5.
- Regulatory pharmacology: [Kineret](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=d9d74915-6606-4570-9c52-c4001d3177de) blocks IL-1 receptor signaling and has pediatric indications in other rare diseases, not MVA.
- OUR INFERENCE: this pathway may be relevant in the modeled genotype. That bridge must be tested.

Cell-surface IL1A matters in the fibroblast mechanism; testing only soluble IL-1 can miss the circuit. Measure receptor dependence and downstream function. A lower cytokine level alone is not a tissue-rescue endpoint.

## Comparison and risks

Rapamycin also affects the secretory circuit (PMID 26147250; DOI 10.1038/ncb3195). Specificity is a reason to compare anakinra with sirolimus, not evidence of superior safety or efficacy. A public competitor report mentioned sirolimus/everolimus, but a non-exhaustive originality check is not a reason to discard a scientifically justified candidate or claim anakinra is unique.

Infection, neutropenia, hypersensitivity and renal clearance require consideration; nephrocalcinosis does not prove renal failure. Monitor inappropriate cell-cycle re-entry because inflammatory/senescence networks may also reinforce arrest. Genetic senescent-cell removal, modulation of secretion and pharmacological senolysis are different interventions. Do not attribute the Ercc1 dasatinib/quercetin study to BubR1 models (PMID 25754370; DOI 10.1111/acel.12344).

## Discriminating experiment

Compare a genotype model with corrected controls, vehicle, anakinra and sirolimus. Add orthogonal IL1A/IL1R perturbation. Separate secretome-producing cells from muscle recipient cells and control drug carryover. Require improved muscle differentiation/fusion or function, with errors per division, cell death and arrest measured. Stop if IL-1 dependence is absent, tissue benefit fails or CIN worsens.

Public transcriptomic datasets GSE134780 and GSE134781 could support or weaken pathway plausibility. They have not been reanalyzed in this project; transcript correlations would not demonstrate drug response. All biological validation remains proposed. Distribute this note with the main report and its required acknowledgment.
